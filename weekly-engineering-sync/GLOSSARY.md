# Weekly Engineering Sync

The FOC weekly engineering sync meeting and the shared Google Doc that holds its agenda and notes, one tab per meeting.

## Language

**Meeting tab**:
A tab in the sync doc, titled with the meeting date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, that holds the agenda and notes for one meeting.
_Avoid_: week tab, sync tab, page

**Prepare for next meeting**:
The operation that turns a freshly duplicated Meeting tab into a blank agenda for the next meeting while carrying forward last meeting's plans as this meeting's expectations.
_Avoid_: roll over, reset, new week

**Workstream table**:
A table in a Meeting tab where one workstream reports per project on what was expected, what actually happened, and the plan until the next meeting.
_Avoid_: workstream update table, status table

**Expected column**:
The workstream table column holding last meeting's plan, carried forward unedited so this meeting can compare it with what actually happened.
_Avoid_: last week's plan, copied column
